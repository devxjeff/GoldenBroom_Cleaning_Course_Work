Our Story

About Golden Broom

A Koforidua business built on trust, quality, and a commitment to greener homes.

How We Started

Built Right Here in Koforidua

Golden Broom was born from a simple observation: the families and professionals of Koforidua needed a home cleaning service they could actually trust — one that showed up on time, did the job properly, and used products that were safe for their children and their environment.

We started small, serving a handful of households in the Eastern Region. Every job was treated with the same care and attention — because we understood that when someone lets you into their home, they are placing real trust in you.

Today, Golden Broom offers a full range of cleaning and home services — from routine weekly cleans to deep sanitisation, laundry, lawn care, and pest control — all delivered by trained professionals with eco-friendly products.
